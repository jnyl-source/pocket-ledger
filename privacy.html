Pocket Ledger — Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

Pocket Ledger does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything you enter stays on the device you entered it on.

What the app stores

Your entries, pockets, currency rates and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else, and the developer cannot see them.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app asks for no permissions.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties, because no data ever leaves your device.

Network use

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts when a connection is available, which involves no information about you or your entries beyond the ordinary network request. If you export a CSV file, that file goes wherever you choose to send it, and that choice is yours alone.

Deleting your data

Use "Erase all data" in the app, or uninstall it. Either removes everything. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, deletion cannot be undone, so export a CSV first if you want to keep a copy.

Children

The app is a personal budgeting tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for consent.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer at jennil.roong@gmail.com.
